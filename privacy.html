Privacy notice

Privacy, in plain language.

MyCommunidad collects your display name, email, phone number, useful address, barangay, report photos, and optional report-location coordinates to operate community issue reporting.

What is public

Public community reports show the issue details, general reported location, status, photos, and an appropriate display name or “Community Member.” They never show your phone number, email, or registered home address.

Who can access private information

Private profile information is restricted to you and authorized personnel when necessary for handling a report. Access is enforced by Firebase Security Rules, not merely hidden in the interface.

Photos and location

Photos support the issue report. GPS is optional and only refers to the reported community issue—not your home location. Avoid capturing people, plates, or other unnecessary personal information.
